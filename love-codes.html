Frequently asked questions

What does 520 mean?

520 (wǔ èr líng) sounds like 我爱你 wǒ ài nǐ, “I love you”. May 20 has become an unofficial Valentine’s Day in China.

What does 1314 mean?

一生一世 “for a whole lifetime”. 5201314 means “I love you for a lifetime”.

What does 3344 mean?

生生世世 “life after life, forever” — a rare case where 4 is positive.

What does 77 mean?

亲亲 “kiss kiss”, and 七夕 Qixi — the 7th day of the 7th lunar month, Chinese Valentine’s Day.

Which number codes should I avoid?

250 (idiot), 748 (go die), 7456 (I’m furious) and anything with 14 (要死). Our dictionary tags these “Avoid”.
