Frequently asked questions

What does 33077 mean in Chinese?

Read as number slang, 3 = 想 (miss), 0 = 你 (you) and 77 = 亲亲 (kiss kiss), so 33077 becomes 想想你亲亲 — “thinking of you, kiss kiss.” It also joins China’s two love festivals: lunar 3/3 (Shangsi) and 7/7 (Qixi). The phrase is a new code built from established parts.

Why do Chinese people care so much about numbers?

Chinese has many words that sound alike, so digits pick up the meaning of words they sound like: 8 (bā) sounds like 发 “prosper”, 4 (sì) like 死 “death”. It affects prices, phone numbers, plates, addresses and wedding dates.

What is the luckiest number in Chinese culture?

8 is the most sought after, followed by 6 (smooth) and 9 (long-lasting). The Beijing Olympics opened at 8:08 pm on 8/8/2008.

Is the lucky score scientific?

No — it measures cultural perception, which is what buyers pay for. Use it for fun, naming and pricing ideas, not as financial advice.
